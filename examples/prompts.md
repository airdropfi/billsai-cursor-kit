# Example prompts

Paste any of these into Cursor (Agent) after the `billsai` server shows a green dot in Customize, then MCPs. All Bills AI tools are read-only.

1. `Convert this bank statement PDF to CSV and show me any warnings.`
2. `I have a 6 MB statement PDF. Get an upload URL, upload it with curl and give me the CSV.`
3. `Which of my saved statements have finished processing, and how many transactions does each have?`
4. `Summarize my income, expenses and savings rate for last year from my saved statements.`
5. `Where did my money go in March? Show spending by category.`
6. `List my saved transactions in the Dining category this year.`
7. `Search my saved transactions for "subscription" and show date and amount.`
8. `Export my saved transactions for June as CSV.`
9. `Show my saved transactions from the statement uploaded most recently.`
10. `Is this PDF something the converter can read, or is it a scanned document? Tell me what to do if it is scanned.`
